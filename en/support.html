01Health and safety boundaries

Health and safety

Seek immediate medical help for these signs Abnormal breathing, blue face or lips, persistent high fever, seizures, severe dehydration, unusually poor responsiveness, inability to feed, unusual medication reactions, injuries, or other emergency signals. Call your local emergency number or go to urgent care; do not rely on app statistics, reminders, or growth percentiles to decide.

Temperature and medicine records do not generate medical reminders automatically. WHO growth references only organize measurements and never provide a diagnosis.

03Frequently asked questions

FAQ

What if I enter a record incorrectly?
Open the record detail to view, edit, or delete it. Changes save locally and update according to sync rules when iCloud or Family Sharing is enabled.
Can timers be paused?
Nursing, sleep, bath, and outdoor timers support pause, resume, and finish. Timer state is persisted and can recover after the app unexpectedly exits. Live Activity display depends on system settings and device support.
Does the app read my whole photo library?
No. Photos are selected through the system photo picker. Until you choose an image, the app does not read your library. After selection, only that image is copied into private local storage.
How do WHO growth percentiles work?
First optionally add birth date and reference sex in Settings, then enter weight, height, or head circumference. The app uses monthly L/M/S data from WHO Child Growth Standards to calculate percentiles and Z-scores, switching from length to stature references after 24 months. Results organize measurements and are not a diagnosis.
Why do reminders cover only some records?
The current version supports feeding-interval and to-do reminders, and can write reminders to the system Calendar. Health records remain manual to avoid automatic medical judgments. You can turn notifications off in system settings.
How do I export records before changing phones?
Go to Settings → Data & Backup → Export Records CSV. Export includes record time, type, values, details, and notes. A complete backup of baby profiles, photos, and documents is still being improved. When changing devices, prefer iCloud restoration with the same Apple ID.
Why has iCloud sync not started?
Personal mode stores data locally with SwiftData and mirrors it to your own private iCloud database. Family Sharing uses a CloudKit shared zone after you create or join a family. Sync requires iCloud sign-in, network availability, and a valid Family subscription state.
