06Data and backups

Data and backups

Records and photos are stored on your device by default. After iCloud sync is enabled, personal data syncs through your own iCloud account (CloudKit); family data collaborates through a CloudKit shared zone in the family owner’s iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details. You can export records as CSV; before changing devices, resetting, or uninstalling, check your iCloud state and keep your own copies of photos and documents. Without iCloud or network access, local data remains available.

Export regularly The app provides CSV record export in Settings. Before transferring, resetting, or uninstalling, export the CSV and separately preserve photos and documents.

For detailed data-processing scope, see the Privacy Policy.

08Purchases and features

Purchases and features

No account is required. Basic logging is free. Paid features include Plus Monthly, Plus Annual, Plus Lifetime, and Family Annual, all paid through Apple In-App Purchase. Plus Monthly/Annual may offer a 7-day free trial when your Apple account is eligible; Family Annual and Lifetime have no trial. Billing, renewal, trial conversion, pre-billing reminders, and refunds follow Apple and the App Store. You can cancel subscriptions in App Store account settings.

Family Annual includes in-app sharing for up to 6 members: 1 creator plus 5 invited caregivers. Family members do not need separate Plus. Family cloud data primarily counts against the owner’s iCloud storage; members must be signed in to their own iCloud account but do not need Apple Family Sharing or shared iCloud+. The developer cannot access shared record content; the public invitation directory keeps only minimized invitation business fields as described in the Privacy Policy. If Family expires or is cancelled, collaboration and member Plus pause; saved records are not deleted.

Subscriptions are also governed by the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).
